Concurrent Programming — Synchronization

Concurrent programs allow multiple tasks to make progress during overlapping periods of time.

Shared mutable state introduces the possibility of race conditions when operations interleave unexpectedly.

Mutexes and locks provide mutual exclusion. Read-write locks allow multiple readers to proceed concurrently while writers require exclusive access.

Deadlocks can occur when tasks wait indefinitely for resources held by one another.

Thread-safe design requires clear ownership, careful synchronization, and well-defined lifecycle behavior.
